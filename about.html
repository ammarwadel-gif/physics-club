About BS Physics Club

A student-run community that treats physics as something to learn, question, build, and compete in.

Who we are

BS Physics Club is a student-organized STEM community that supports learners at every stage — from a first encounter with Newton's laws to an independent research project or an international olympiad attempt. We operate as an academic ecosystem within the school's STEM community, not just a single weekly meeting.

Our mission

To make physics learning, research, experimentation, and academic opportunity more accessible, by organizing structured resources and clear pathways instead of scattered handouts and one-off events.

Our vision

To be the place a student comes to learn physics deeply, run a real experiment, start a research project, and find the right competition to test what they've built — all within one academic community.

What we do

  • Weekly academic sessions and topic reviews
  • A structured Learning Hub with per-semester checklists
  • Research education and mentorship
  • Simulation-based and physical experiments
  • Competition preparation and a live competitions database
  • Seasonal academic programs and research tracks

Our academic pillars

Four connected pillars carry a student from first principles to independent research and competition.

PILLAR 01

Learning

Structured, filterable resources across every core physics unit.

PILLAR 02

Research

Methodology, tools, and mentorship for independent investigation.

PILLAR 03

Experimental Science

Simulation-based and physical experiments, fully documented.

PILLAR 04

Competitions

A living database of opportunities matched to student level.
