About Physics Club

A student-run community that treats physics as something to learn, question, build, and compete in.

Who we are

Physics Club is a student-organized STEM community that supports learners at every stage — from a first encounter with Newton's laws to an independent research project or an international olympiad attempt.

Our mission

To make physics learning, research, experimentation, and academic opportunity more accessible, by organizing structured resources and clear pathways.

Our vision

To be the place a student comes to learn physics deeply, run a real experiment, start a research project, and find the right competition to test what they've built.

What we do

  • Weekly academic sessions and topic reviews
  • A structured Learning Hub
  • Research education and mentorship
  • Simulation-based and physical experiments
  • Competition preparation and a live database
  • Seasonal academic programs
