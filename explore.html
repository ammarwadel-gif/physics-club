Explore Our System

A complete guide to the BS Physics Club academic ecosystem — what each section does, and how to make the most of your journey here.

What you'll find here

Seven connected pillars carry a student from first principles to independent research and competition.

📚 Pillar 01

Learning Hub

Structured physics content organized by grade and semester. Each Learning Objective comes with sessions, files, and quizzes — all tracked with a personal checklist.

Open Learning Hub 🔬 Pillar 02

Research Hub

A growing library of research topics. Every topic holds session recordings, reference files, and practical tasks to help you move from reading papers to conducting your own investigation.

Explore Research 🏆 Pillar 03

Competitions

A searchable database of physics competitions — filtered by scope (International, National, Local), with eligibility, deadlines, and preparation resources for each one.

Browse Competitions 🎓 Pillar 04

Programs

Seasonal academic tracks run by the club — competition prep, research mentorship, computational physics workshops, and more.

View Programs 📰 Pillar 05

Magazine

Club-published articles, features, and stories from the world of physics — written by students, for students.

Read Magazine 📖 Pillar 06

Books

Curated books and reading recommendations to deepen your understanding of physics at every level.

Browse Books 🌍 Pillar 07

Community & About

How to get started

Three simple steps to begin your journey through the ecosystem.

STEP 01

Open the Learning Hub

Pick your grade and semester, then browse the Learning Objectives. Track your progress as you complete sessions.

STEP 02

Pick a competition or program

Browse competitions by scope or explore seasonal programs. Each one lists eligibility, deadlines, and application steps.

STEP 03

Start researching

Dive into the Research Hub to explore sessions, files, and tasks on topics that interest you.
